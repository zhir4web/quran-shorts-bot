Quran Bot

Quran Bot is a private tool used by the Quran Shorts channel owner to prepare and manage Quran recitation videos.

What the app does

The app creates short Quran recitation videos from the channel owner's selected recitation and licensed visual assets. It can upload and publish videos to the owner's YouTube channel, organize videos into playlists, add a short channel comment, and read channel and video performance data so the owner can check uploads and reporting. The owner can also request a video to be delivered to their TikTok inbox as an unpublished draft; the owner reviews and publishes TikTok drafts themselves.

Why Google access is requested

The channel owner connects a Google account so the app can confirm the selected YouTube channel, upload videos and manage related playlists and comments, and read video statistics and YouTube Analytics for operational reports. Google account data is used only for these channel-management features. The app does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Policies and contact

Read the Privacy Policy for details about Google and TikTok data, storage, sharing, and retention. The Terms of Service explain the conditions for using this owner-operated tool. For questions, contact the project maintainer through the Quran Shorts project page. Never include passwords or access tokens in a public issue.
